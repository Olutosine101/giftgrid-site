Returns & refunds.

If something is wrong with your gift box, tell us within 24 hours of delivery and we will replace it or refund you. Last updated 23 November 2026.

The short version

  • Damaged, missing or wrong items: we replace them or refund their value, at no cost to you. You do not need to send anything back.
  • Cancellations: full refund if you cancel before we start packing your order.
  • Late or failed delivery caused by us: free redelivery, or a full refund if you prefer.
  • Change of mind after delivery: because our boxes contain food and fresh produce, we cannot accept returns of items that are not damaged or wrong.

Damaged, missing or wrong items

We check every box before it leaves us, but if anything arrives damaged, spoiled, missing or different from what you ordered:

  1. Contact us within 24 hours of delivery on WhatsApp (+234 808 944 0743) or at hello@giftgrid.com.ng.
  2. Send your order number and a photo of the item or box.
  3. We will deliver a replacement item (an exchange) or refund the value of the item, whichever you choose. If a replacement is not available, we refund it.

You do not need to return the damaged or wrong item, and there is no fee for a replacement or refund. The recipient can contact us directly if you are sending from abroad.

Change of mind

Our gift boxes contain food, drinks and fresh produce. For hygiene and food-safety reasons, we cannot take back or refund items that arrive in good condition and as ordered. If you are unsure about a box, message us before you order and we will help you choose.

Cancelling or changing an order

  • Before we start packing: cancel for a full refund, or change the delivery date, address or message for free. Orders are usually packed the day before delivery.
  • After packing has started: we cannot cancel the order, but we will do our best to change the delivery date or address. A new delivery fee may apply if the address is in a different area.
  • Custom and corporate orders: can be cancelled for a full refund before we buy the items for your order. After that, cancellation terms are as agreed in your quote.

Late or failed delivery

If we deliver later than the date and time window you chose, or cannot deliver because of a problem on our side, we will redeliver at no cost or give you a full refund, including delivery.

If delivery fails because the address is incorrect or the recipient and the backup contact cannot be reached, we will contact you to arrange a new delivery. In that case, a second delivery fee may apply. Fresh items that cannot be held may not be refundable.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, through Paystack.
  • We start your refund within 2 working days of approving it.
  • Depending on your bank, it can take 5 to 10 working days to show in your account. Refunds to cards issued outside Nigeria can take longer.
  • Refunds are made in naira, the currency you paid in. If you paid with a foreign card, the amount you receive may differ slightly because of your bank's exchange rate.

Contact us

For any issue with an order, message us on WhatsApp (+234 808 944 0743) or email hello@giftgrid.com.ng. We reply the same day. See also delivery areas and fees and our FAQ.
