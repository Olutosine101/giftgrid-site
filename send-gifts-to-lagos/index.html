Questions from abroad

Can I pay from the UK, US or Canada?

Prices are in naira and you pay securely through Paystack. If your foreign card is declined, message us on WhatsApp and we will send another way to pay.

How will I know it was delivered?

You get an email receipt when you pay, and we message you on WhatsApp when the box leaves and when it arrives.

Can I choose the delivery date?

Yes. Pick any date at checkout. Order by 9:00 a.m. Lagos time the day before.

What do people usually send home?

The Family Care & Support foodstuff hamper is the most-sent gift to parents. Fruit baskets and breakfast boxes are popular for birthdays.
