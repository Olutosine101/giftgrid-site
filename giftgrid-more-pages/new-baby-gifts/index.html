How ordering works

  1. Pick a box from the collection, or ask us to curate one on WhatsApp.

Questions, answered

What should I send a new mum in Lagos?

Fresh fruit and a foodstuff hamper are the most useful gifts in the first weeks. Add a warm note on the card.

Can you deliver to a hospital after the birth?

Yes. Add the ward details and a family contact. Check the hospital allows deliveries.

Do you make gifts for naming ceremonies?

Yes. Ask on WhatsApp for souvenir boxes in bulk.
