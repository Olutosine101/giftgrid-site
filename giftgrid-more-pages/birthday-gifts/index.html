The easiest birthday surprise in Lagos

A birthday gift that arrives at the door says you planned ahead, even if you ordered yesterday. Pick the box, choose their birthday as the delivery date, add a message for the card, and we handle the rest.

Which birthday box should you choose?

  • Start their day right: a breakfast box such as the Pancake Tower or Continental Royalty, delivered between 7:30 and 11:00 a.m.
  • For the one with a sweet tooth: the Sweet Tooth & Craving box of chocolates, doughnuts and snacks.
  • For a partner: the Love Language Box with red roses, chocolates and a scented candle.
  • For someone who has everything: the Coffee & Tea Connoisseur or a fresh exotic fruit crate.
  • For a big birthday: ask us for a custom box built around their favourite things.

How ordering works

  1. Pick a box from the collection, or ask us to curate one on WhatsApp.
  2. Choose the delivery date and time window. Order by 12 noon the day before.
  3. Add their details and a card message, then pay by card, transfer or USSD.

Make it a surprise

Add the recipient's phone number and a backup contact (a gate, reception or flatmate) so the rider can reach them without spoiling the surprise. Tell us in the notes if we should not call ahead.

Questions, answered

Can you deliver on the actual birthday?

Yes. Choose the birthday as the delivery date at checkout. Order by 12 noon the day before.

Can I add a birthday message?

Yes. Type your message in the card message field at checkout and we print it on the gift card.

Can you keep it a surprise?

Yes. Add a backup contact and a note such as "please don't call before arriving" and the rider will follow it.

Do you deliver birthday gifts outside Lagos?

Online orders cover Lagos. For other cities, chat with us on WhatsApp.
