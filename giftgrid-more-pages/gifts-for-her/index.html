Finding the right gift for her

The best gifts feel chosen for one person. Think about how she likes to be treated: a slow breakfast, something sweet, something healthy, or something romantic. Then let the delivery do the talking.

Ideas by who she is

  • Wife or girlfriend: the Love Language Box with fresh red roses, Ferrero Rocher, truffles and a scented candle.
  • Mum: a Family Care hamper stocked with essentials, or fresh fruit. See gifts for mum.
  • Best friend or sister: breakfast in bed with the Pancake Tower, or a treat box.
  • Colleague or boss: the Coffee & Tea Connoisseur feels premium and fits any office.
  • Health-conscious: the Exotic Wellness Crate of dragon fruit, strawberries, kiwi and raw honey.

How ordering works

  1. Pick a box from the collection, or ask us to curate one on WhatsApp.

Yes. Add the office address, her name and a reception contact. Choose a time window during office hours.

Yes. Your card message is printed and placed in the box.
