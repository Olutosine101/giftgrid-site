Romantic gifts that arrive on the day

Whether it is your first anniversary or your twentieth, a gift that turns up at the door feels like an event. Choose the date, write the card, and let the box do the rest.

  • The romantic box: the Love Language Box with Four Cousins sweet red wine, Ferrero Rocher, truffles, a scented candle and fresh red roses.

How ordering works

  1. Pick a box from the collection, or ask us to curate one on WhatsApp.
  2. Choose the delivery date and time window. Order by 12 noon the day before.
  3. Add their details and a card message, then pay by card, transfer or USSD.

Yes. Add the office address and a reception contact. Note that the Love Language Box contains wine.

Yes. Ask on WhatsApp and we will quote for extra roses.
