Mothering Sunday 2027 is on 7 March

In Nigeria, Mother's Day is celebrated on Mothering Sunday, three weeks before Easter. In 2027 that is Sunday 7 March. Order by 12 noon on Saturday 6 March, or earlier to be safe.

Gifts mums love

  • Stock her kitchen: the Family Care & Support hamper.
  • Fresh fruit: the Exotic Wellness Crate or Harvest Abundance Basket.
  • Breakfast after church: the Continental Royalty, delivered 7:30 to 11:00 a.m.

How ordering works

  1. Pick a box from the collection, or ask us to curate one on WhatsApp.
  2. Choose the delivery date and time window. Order by 12 noon the day before.
  3. Add their details and a card message, then pay by card, transfer or USSD.

Questions, answered

When is Mother's Day in Nigeria in 2027?

Sunday 7 March 2027 (Mothering Sunday).

Can I send a Mother's Day gift from abroad?

Yes. Pay by card online and add her Lagos address. See sending from abroad.

Can I order for several mums at once?

Yes. Place one order per address, or send us the list on WhatsApp.
