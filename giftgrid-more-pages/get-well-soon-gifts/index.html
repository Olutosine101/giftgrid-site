Gifts that help someone recover

When someone is unwell, the best gifts are fresh, easy to share and take a job off the family's plate. Fruit baskets are the classic choice; a foodstuff hamper helps the family at home.

  • Fresh fruit: the Exotic Wellness Crate with raw honey, or the Harvest Abundance Basket.
  • For the family at home: the Family Care & Support hamper.
  • For a long recovery: the Coffee & Tea Connoisseur for visitors and slow afternoons.

Delivering to a hospital

Add the hospital name, ward or room, and a family member's number who can come to the gate or reception. Check that the hospital allows food deliveries, and tell us about any diet the patient is on.

How ordering works

  1. Pick a box from the collection, or ask us to curate one on WhatsApp.
  2. Choose the delivery date and time window. Order by 12 noon the day before.
  3. Add their details and a card message, then pay by card, transfer or USSD.

Questions, answered

Can you deliver to a hospital in Lagos?

Yes. Add the ward or room details and a contact who can receive the gift. Check the hospital allows deliveries.

Can I leave out certain foods?

Yes. Add a note at checkout, or message us on WhatsApp before ordering.

How fresh is the fruit?

Fruit baskets are packed on the day of delivery with fruit bought that morning or the day before.
