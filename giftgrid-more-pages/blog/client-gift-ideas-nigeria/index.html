Client Gift Ideas in Nigeria That Don't Look Generic

GiftGrid · October 28, 2026

Every December, clients receive diaries, calendars and branded mugs. Few of them are remembered. A client gift that feels thoughtful does three things: it is genuinely useful or delightful, it arrives at the right time, and it carries a personal note.

Gifts clients actually keep using

Coffee & Tea Connoisseur · ₦55,000Premium coffee and tea for their desk or home. Our top client gift.
Exotic Wellness Crate · ₦65,000A fresh exotic fruit crate that the whole office can share.
Family Care & Support · ₦85,000For your most important relationships: a generous hamper for their home.

Budget per client

  • Key accounts: ₦55,000 to ₦85,000, such as a coffee box or Family Care hamper.
  • Wider client list: ₦35,000 to ₦45,000, such as a fruit basket or treat box.
  • From 10 boxes you get volume pricing; see corporate gifting.

Make it personal, not generic

  • Write a different note for your top clients. One specific sentence about the year together is enough.
  • Use a branded note card rather than branding the whole gift.
  • Deliver to the person, not just the company: their office desk or, with permission, their home.

Timing

Gifts that arrive in the first two weeks of December stand out before the rush. New Year gifts in early January are a smart alternative, when inboxes are quiet. For Christmas delivery, confirm corporate orders by 11 December.
