What Goes Into a GiftGrid Box: Packaging, Brands and Care

GiftGrid · October 28, 2026

A gift box should feel special the moment it arrives. Here is what goes into every GiftGrid box, from the packaging to the delivery.

Packaging made for the gift

  • Clear-lid gift boxes for breakfast, so the spread is visible the moment it arrives.
  • Woven tray baskets for fruit, so the basket can be kept and reused.
  • Kraft gift boxes for treats and coffee.
  • Large cloth bags for foodstuff hampers, strong enough for rice, oil and yam.
  • A black gift box for the romantic Love Language Box.

Brands people know

We fill boxes with familiar names: Nescafé, Lipton, McVitie's, Ferrero Rocher, Kinder, Pringles, Mama Gold, Kings, Golden Penny, Chivita and more, alongside fresh fruit and freshly made breakfast. Boxes are put together in Lagos.

Fresh things, made fresh

Breakfast boxes are prepared on the morning of delivery and arrive between 7:30 and 11:00 a.m. Fruit baskets are packed on the day of delivery with fruit bought that morning or the day before.

Your words, on a card

Every order has a card message field at checkout. Your message goes in the box.

From our door to theirs

We deliver to 51 areas across Lagos, in the time window you choose. You can see every area and fee on our delivery page.
