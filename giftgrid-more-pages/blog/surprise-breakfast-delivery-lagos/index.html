Surprise Breakfast Delivery in Lagos: How It Works

GiftGrid · October 28, 2026

A surprise breakfast is one of the most loved gifts in Lagos. It arrives early, it is shared on the spot, and it turns an ordinary morning into a moment. Here is exactly how it works with GiftGrid.

1. Choose a breakfast box

The Continental Royalty · ₦48,000Belgian waffles, mini pancakes, eggs, chicken franks, a club sandwich, fruit and orange juice.
Pancake Tower · ₦42,000Six buttermilk pancakes with strawberries, Nutella, maple syrup, Biscoff, turkey bacon and iced coffee.
Sweet & Savoury Naija Morning · ₦40,000Fried yam, egg sauce, dodo, peppered chicken wings, akara and creamy pap.

The Continental Royalty is the most complete, the Pancake Tower is the sweetest, and the Naija Morning is for anyone who would rather have yam and akara than waffles.

2. Pick the date and time window

Breakfast boxes are delivered between 7:30 and 11:00 a.m. Choose one of three windows at checkout: 7:30 to 8:30, 8:30 to 9:30, or 9:30 to 11:00 a.m. Order by 12 noon the day before.

3. Keep it a surprise

  • Use the recipient's phone number, but add a backup contact such as a flatmate, gate or estate security.
  • In the notes, tell us not to call ahead if you want the knock on the door to be the surprise.
  • Write your message in the card field. It is printed and placed in the box.

4. Pay and relax

Pay by card, transfer or USSD through Paystack. You will get an email receipt straight away.
