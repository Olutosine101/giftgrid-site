What to Send Someone in Hospital in Lagos: A Get Well Soon Gift Guide

GiftGrid · October 28, 2026

When someone is unwell, a gift says "I am thinking of you" when you cannot be there. The right gift is fresh, easy to share and makes life a little easier for the family looking after them.

What to think about first

  • Diet: some patients are on special diets. Ask the family, and tell us in the notes what to leave out.
  • Rules: check that the hospital allows food deliveries to the ward.
  • Sharing: visitors and nurses often share gifts, so generous is better than fancy.

How to deliver to a hospital

  1. Add the hospital name and address.
  2. Add the ward or room number in the address details.
  3. Use a family member's phone number as the contact, so someone can come to reception.
  4. Choose a daytime window during visiting hours.

What to write on the card

Keep it warm and short: "Thinking of you and wishing you a quick recovery", or a line only the two of you would understand.
