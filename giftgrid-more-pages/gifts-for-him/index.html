Gifts men actually enjoy

Most men like gifts they can use or eat straight away. A big breakfast, good coffee or a box of snacks for the weekend lands better than another ornament.

Ideas by who he is

  • Husband or boyfriend: a surprise breakfast like the Sweet & Savoury Naija Morning (fried yam, egg sauce, dodo, peppered chicken wings and akara).
  • Dad: a practical Family Care hamper, or the Coffee & Tea Connoisseur for his morning cup.
  • Brother or friend: the Sweet Tooth & Craving box for match day.
  • Colleague or client: see corporate gifts for bulk orders with one invoice.

How ordering works

  1. Pick a box from the collection, or ask us to curate one on WhatsApp.
  2. Choose the delivery date and time window. Order by 12 noon the day before.
  3. Add their details and a card message, then pay by card, transfer or USSD.

Questions, answered

What is a good gift for a man in Lagos?

Breakfast boxes and the Coffee & Tea Connoisseur are our most popular gifts for men. For dads, a Family Care hamper is practical and generous.

Can you deliver to his workplace?

Yes. Add the office address and a reception contact, and pick a time window during office hours.

Can I build a custom box for him?

Yes. Tell us on WhatsApp what he likes and your budget.
