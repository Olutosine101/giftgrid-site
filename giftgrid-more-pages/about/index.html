About GiftGrid

Gifting in Lagos, made easy.

GiftGrid is a Lagos gift-box company. We curate, pack and deliver gift boxes and hampers for birthdays, anniversaries, Christmas, corporate gifting and every reason to show up for someone.

What we do

  • Curated boxes: breakfast boxes, fruit baskets, foodstuff hampers, treat boxes, coffee boxes and romantic boxes, with brands people know.
  • Custom boxes: tell us the occasion and budget on WhatsApp and we build a box around it.
  • Corporate gifting: staff and client gifts in bulk, with branded note cards and one invoice.
  • Delivery across Lagos: to 51 areas, from Ikeja and Gbagada to Lekki, Victoria Island, Ikoyi and Ajah, on the date and in the time window you choose.

How it works

  1. Pick a box, or ask us to curate one.
  2. Choose the delivery date and time window. Order by 12 noon the day before.
  3. Add their details and a card message, and pay securely by card, transfer or USSD via Paystack.

You can order from Lagos or from anywhere in the world, as international cards are accepted.
