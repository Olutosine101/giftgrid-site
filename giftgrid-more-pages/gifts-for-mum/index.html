  1. Pick a box from the collection, or ask us to curate one on WhatsApp.

Questions, answered

What should I send my mum in Lagos?

The Family Care & Support hamper is the most-sent gift to parents. Fruit baskets and breakfast boxes are popular for birthdays.

When is Mothering Sunday 2027?

Mothering Sunday in Nigeria is on 7 March 2027. See our Mother's Day gifts page.

Can I pay from the UK or US?

Yes. Paystack accepts international Visa and Mastercard cards.

Will someone call her before delivery?

The rider calls the recipient's number when close. Add a second number in case hers is off.
