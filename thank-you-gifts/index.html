Thank you, said properly

A message is nice. A box that arrives at their desk or door, with your thank-you on the card, is remembered.

  • For a colleague or client: the Coffee & Tea Connoisseur.
  • For a host or neighbour: the Harvest Abundance Basket of fresh fruit and juice.
  • For a team: treat boxes or Staff Appreciation Boxes; see corporate gifting for bulk orders.
  • For a teacher, nurse or doctor: the Sweet Tooth & Craving box to share with the staff room.

How ordering works

  1. Pick a box from the collection, or ask us to curate one on WhatsApp.
  2. Choose the delivery date and time window. Order by 12 noon the day before.
  3. Add their details and a card message, then pay by card, transfer or USSD.

Questions, answered

What is a good thank you gift for a client?

The Coffee & Tea Connoisseur is our most popular client thank-you. For several clients, see corporate gifting for volume pricing.

Can you deliver to an office?

Yes. Add the office address and a reception contact, and choose a time window during office hours.

Can I write a thank you note?

Yes. Add it in the card message field at checkout.
