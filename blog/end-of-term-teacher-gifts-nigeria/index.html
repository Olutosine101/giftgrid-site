End-of-Term Gifts for Teachers in Nigeria: Ideas Parents Can Afford

GiftGrid · November 17, 2026

As the first term ends in December, many parents want to thank the teachers who look after their children every day. A teacher's gift does not need to be expensive. It needs to be useful, fair and sent at the right time. Here are ideas that work for every budget.

First, check the school's policy

Some schools have rules about gifts to staff, such as a maximum value or a preference for group gifts. Check with the school or the class parents' group before you buy.

The class group gift

When every parent in a class contributes a small amount, the teacher can receive something genuinely generous, and no family feels pressure to compete. One parent collects the money and places a single order.

Staff Gold Hamper · ₦103,000A larger store-up with a 10kg bag of rice, for a group gift from the whole class.

A small personal thank-you

If you are giving on your own, a treat or a fruit basket is warm and appropriate.

Sweet Tooth & Craving · ₦40,000Chocolates, doughnuts, biscuits, chin-chin and candied cashews.
Harvest Abundance Basket · ₦35,000A fresh fruit basket with pineapple, watermelon, grapes and juice.

Don't forget the other staff

Class assistants, nannies, drivers on the school bus and security staff help your child too. A simple pantry pack for each of them is a kind and affordable gesture.

Staff Starter Pack · ₦34,000Rice, oil, pasta, noodles and seasoning: a practical thank-you.

Budget guide

  • From one family: ₦35,000 to ₦40,000 for a fruit basket or treat box.
  • From the class: ₦69,500 to ₦103,000 for a staff pack or store-up hamper.
  • For support staff: ₦34,000 per person for a pantry pack.

Timing and delivery

  • Send it before the last day of term, so the teacher receives it before travelling for the holidays.
  • Ask whether to deliver to the school or to the teacher's home. Heavy foodstuff hampers are easier to receive at home.
  • Include a note from the children. A short message or the class's names makes it personal.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good end-of-term gift for a teacher in Nigeria?

A class group gift such as a foodstuff hamper is the most useful. From one family, a fruit basket or treat box is a warm, affordable thank-you.

How much should parents spend on a teacher's gift?

Around ₦35,000 to ₦40,000 from one family, or pool money as a class for a larger hamper. Always check the school's gift policy first.

Can you deliver to a school in Lagos?

Yes, or to the teacher's home. For heavy foodstuff hampers, the home is usually easier. Choose the date before the last day of term.
