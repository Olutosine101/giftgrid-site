End-of-Year Staff Gift Ideas Nigerian Teams Will Actually Love

GiftGrid · October 22, 2026

End-of-year gifts are how a company says "we saw your effort this year". The best staff gifts in Nigeria are useful, generous and a little bit special. Here are end-of-year gift ideas that teams actually love, with rough budgets for each.

1. The foodstuff hamper

The classic Nigerian staff Christmas gift, for good reason. Rice, oil, seasoning and drinks help every household over the festive season. Practical gifts are remembered longest.

Staff Appreciation Box · ₦38,000The practical favourite, around ₦38,000 per person.

2. The coffee and tea box

For office teams and clients, a coffee and tea box feels premium without being extravagant. It works well as a client gift too.

Coffee & Tea Connoisseur · ₦55,000Nescafé Gold, Cappuccino, Coffee-mate, Lipton, Hobnobs and more.

3. The fruit hamper

A fresh, healthy choice for teams that already get plenty of snacks. Exotic fruit hampers feel generous and suit staff who don't celebrate Christmas.

Exotic Wellness Crate · ₦65,000Dragon fruit, kiwi, pomegranate, grapes and raw honey.

4. The treat box for smaller teams

For start-ups and small teams, a box of chocolates, snacks and pastries delivered to everyone on the same morning makes a fun end-of-year moment.

Sweet Tooth & Craving · ₦40,000Ferrero Rocher, Kinder Bueno, doughnuts, chin-chin and more.

How to make any staff gift feel personal

  • Add a handwritten or printed note from the founder or team lead.
  • Deliver to homes so families share the gift.
  • Time it before people travel, ideally mid December.
  • Give senior staff and top performers a step-up box.
