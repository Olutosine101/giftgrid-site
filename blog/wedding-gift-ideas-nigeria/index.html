Wedding Gift Ideas in Nigeria the Couple Will Actually Use

GiftGrid · November 17, 2026

Nigerian couples often go home from their wedding with a car full of identical gifts: three blenders, two sets of plates, and more wall clocks than walls. The gifts they remember are the ones that help in the first, busy weeks of married life. Here is how to choose one.

The best wedding gifts are practical

After the wedding, a couple is setting up a home, often on a tight budget after paying for the celebration. A gift that stocks their kitchen, or gives them a slow first morning together, is far more welcome than another appliance.

Stock their new home

A foodstuff hamper is the gift couples talk about, because it saves them a big market run in their first month. Have it delivered to their new home a few days after the wedding.

Family Care & Support · ₦85,000Rice, oil, Semovita, eggs, stockfish, dried fish, crayfish, tomato paste and seasoning.
Breakfast Provisions Box · ₦51,000Cornflakes, Golden Morn, oats, Peak milk, Milo, tea, sugar and chocolate spread.

Give them a slow morning

The morning after the wedding, or their first morning back from the honeymoon, is a perfect moment for a surprise breakfast delivered to the door.

The Continental Royalty · ₦48,000Waffles, pancakes, eggs, chicken franks, sandwiches, fruit and juice.
Sweet & Savoury Naija Morning · ₦40,000Fried yam, egg sauce, dodo, peppered chicken wings, akara and pap.

Something romantic

For close friends, a romantic box for the couple's first weeks together is a thoughtful, personal touch.

Love Language Box · ₦75,000Wine (18+), Ferrero Rocher, truffles, a scented candle, roses and a message card.

Budget guide

  • Colleague or acquaintance: ₦40,000 to ₦55,000, such as a breakfast box or breakfast provisions.
  • Friend: ₦55,000 to ₦85,000, such as the Family Care hamper.
  • Close friend or family: Combine a home hamper with a breakfast delivery, or curate a custom box.
  • Group gift: Pool money with friends or colleagues for a store-up hamper.

When and where to deliver

  • Not to the venue. Gifts get lost in the crowd and the couple has to carry everything home.
  • Deliver to their home a few days after the wedding, or on the day they return from their honeymoon.
  • Add a personal message so they know exactly who it came from when they write their thank-you notes.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good wedding gift in Nigeria?

Something practical for the couple's new home, such as a foodstuff or breakfast provisions hamper, or a surprise breakfast after the wedding. These get used, unlike a third blender.

How much should I spend on a wedding gift in Nigeria?

Around ₦40,000 to ₦55,000 for a colleague, ₦55,000 to ₦85,000 for a friend, and more for close family. Group gifts let friends give something bigger together.

Should I bring the gift to the wedding venue?

It is better to have it delivered to the couple's home a few days after the wedding, so nothing gets lost and they do not have to carry it home.
