How to Choose a Gift Box or Hamper Company in Lagos: A Checklist

GiftGrid · November 17, 2026

Lagos has hundreds of gift box and hamper vendors, from Instagram pages to large suppliers. Many are excellent. Some are not. The problem is that you usually only find out on the day the gift arrives, or does not. These ten questions help you choose a vendor before you pay, whether you are sending one birthday box or a hundred staff hampers.

The 10 questions to ask

  1. Can I see the exact item list, with sizes and brands? "Rice, oil, provisions" tells you nothing. "Royal Stallion rice 5kg, King's oil 2L" tells you everything.
  2. Are the photos of real boxes you have packed? Ask for a photo of a recent delivery, not just the catalogue image.
  3. What is the delivery fee, and when will I see it? A good vendor shows the fee before you pay, based on the area.
  4. Can I choose the delivery date and a time window? For birthdays and surprises, timing is everything.
  5. What is the order cut-off? Know the last time you can order for next-day delivery, and the Christmas deadlines.
  6. Do you cover my area? Lagos is big. Check that the vendor delivers to Ikorodu, Ajah or Festac if that is where your person lives.
  7. How will I know it was delivered? You should get a confirmation, ideally with a photo.
  8. What happens if something is wrong? Ask what they do about a missing or damaged item before you need to.
  9. How do I pay, and do I get a receipt? Card and transfer with a proper receipt protects you. For companies, ask for an invoice.
  10. Can you include a personal note or a branded card? The message is often what people remember most.

Red flags

  • Prices that are only shared in DMs, and change depending on who asks.
  • Catalogue photos that look like stock images, with no real deliveries shown.
  • No fixed delivery fee until after you have paid.
  • "Assorted provisions" with no list.
  • No clear answer on what happens if the gift is late.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know a Lagos gift vendor is reliable?

Ask for the exact item list with sizes, photos of real recent deliveries, the delivery fee before payment and what happens if something goes wrong. Clear answers to these are a good sign.

What should a gift box company tell me before I pay?

The full item list, the total price including delivery, the delivery date and time window, how you will be notified of delivery, and your payment receipt or invoice.
