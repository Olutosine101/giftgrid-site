Best Gift Box and Hamper Delivery Services in Lagos (2026)

GiftGrid · November 17, 2026

Lagos has no shortage of places to buy a gift, but they are not all the same kind of business. Some are hamper suppliers built for bulk corporate orders, some are florists, and some are small studios that hand-make surprise boxes. Picking the right type of service matters more than picking a name. This guide compares eight options and explains who each one suits.

A note on fairness: this guide is written by GiftGrid, and we have included ourselves. We have tried to describe every company accurately and neutrally. Details for other companies come from their own websites or public business listings, checked in November 2026. Services and prices change, so always confirm directly before you order.

2. Wigmore Trading

Wigmore Trading describes itself as a supplier of gift hampers for individuals, businesses and retailers, including food staples, beverages, chocolates, wines and imported festive products. Its website offers branded and themed hampers for corporate clients, wholesale pricing, and delivery covering Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt. Orders and quotes go through WhatsApp or email, and its hamper pages do not list fixed prices.

Best for: companies placing large or wholesale hamper orders, especially across several Nigerian cities.
Consider others if: you want to see a price and check out online for a single gift.

3. The Hamper Shop

According to its business listing, The Hamper Shop in Maryland, Lagos offers curated and custom hampers, including gourmet food baskets, premium wines, spa collections and personalised keepsakes, with gift wrapping and an online store you can filter by occasion and price.

Best for: browsing gourmet, wine or spa hampers online by occasion.
Consider others if: you want a foodstuff store-up hamper for a family.

4. Jay's Gift Basket

According to its business listing, Jay's Gift Basket in Yaba makes curated and customised gift baskets for birthdays, anniversaries, holidays and corporate events, with options including gourmet treats, chocolates, wine and spa products.

Best for: a basket built to your own brief, especially if you can visit the store.
Consider others if: you want to order and pay entirely online.

5. Giftshaven NG

Giftshaven NG, based around Ilupeju, offers curated gift boxes, fresh flower arrangements and surprise deliveries for birthdays, anniversaries and romantic occasions, with orders through its Instagram page.

Best for: romantic and birthday surprises with flowers.
Consider others if: you are ordering in bulk for a company.

6. Buyflowers

As reported by TechCabal, Buyflowers sells flower bouquets alongside chocolates, plants, wine, cakes, balloons, books and perfumes, and delivers in Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan, Port Harcourt, Benin and Kaduna, with card, transfer and international payment options.

Best for: flowers with add-ons, or sending to someone outside Lagos.
Consider others if: you want a food or provisions hamper.

7. Prestige Flowershop

Prestige Flowershop sells fresh flowers, luxury arrangements and gift add-ons. Its website says it delivers anywhere in Lagos, is open 24 hours a day, and dispatches orders within 30 to 120 minutes.

Best for: urgent flower orders at any hour.
Consider others if: flowers are not what the person would most enjoy.

8. SureGifts

SureGifts sells gift cards, e-gift cards and vouchers that can be redeemed at partner merchants, delivered by email, SMS or home delivery.

Best for: when you would rather let the person choose for themselves.
Consider others if: you want the gift to feel personal and arrive as something they can open.

How to choose between them

  • Know the type of gift first. Food hamper, flowers, a surprise box or a gift card? That narrows the list immediately.
  • Check delivery coverage. Lagos-only services may suit you better within Lagos; multi-city services help if your person is in Abuja or Port Harcourt.
  • Ask for the full item list and total price, including delivery, before you pay.
  • For companies, ask about invoices, branding and volume pricing.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best hamper company in Lagos?

It depends on what you are sending. For large corporate or wholesale orders, a supplier like Wigmore Trading suits; for fixed-price curated boxes with online checkout across Lagos, GiftGrid; for flowers, a florist like Prestige Flowershop or Buyflowers.

Which gift services deliver outside Lagos?

Wigmore Trading's website lists Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt, and Buyflowers delivers in six cities including Abuja and Port Harcourt. GiftGrid currently delivers within Lagos only.
