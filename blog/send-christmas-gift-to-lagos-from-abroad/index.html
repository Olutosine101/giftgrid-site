How to Send a Christmas Gift to Your Parents in Lagos from the UK, US or Canada

GiftGrid · October 22, 2026

If you are spending Christmas in the UK, US or Canada, a gift delivered to your parents' door in Lagos is the next best thing to being there. Here is how to send a Christmas gift to Lagos from abroad, without relying on a cousin to run around for you.

1. Choose a gift that helps the household

Parents in Lagos appreciate gifts they can use over the festive season. A foodstuff hamper with rice, oil, eggs, fish, seasoning and yam is the most-sent Christmas gift home, and it saves a trip to the market in December traffic.

2. Add something special

Pair the essentials with something that feels like a treat: a fresh fruit basket or a surprise breakfast on Christmas Eve morning.

Harvest Abundance Basket · ₦35,000Pineapple, pawpaw, oranges, watermelon, dates and tiger nuts.

3. Pay by card online

Order on giftgrid.com.ng. Prices are in naira and you pay securely through Paystack with your card. If your card is declined, message us on WhatsApp and we'll arrange another way to pay.

4. Get the delivery details right

  • Use your parent's phone number and the full Lagos address, including a landmark.
  • Add a backup contact, such as a sibling, neighbour or gate security.
  • Write a Christmas message. We put it on a card inside the hamper.

5. Order before the rush

Order by 9:00 a.m. on 23 December for delivery by 24 December. Earlier in December is better: fewer delays and the full range in stock.
