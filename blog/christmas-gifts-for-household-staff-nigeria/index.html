Christmas Gifts for Your Driver, Nanny and Household Staff in Nigeria

GiftGrid · November 17, 2026

The people who drive your children to school, clean your home, cook, and guard the gate are part of your household's year. Christmas is the moment to thank them properly. The most valued gifts are practical ones that help their own families celebrate.

Food first

For most household staff, a foodstuff hamper is the most useful Christmas gift, because it takes care of their family's Christmas cooking. Pair it with their bonus, and the gift feels complete.

For long-serving staff

For someone who has been with your family for many years, a larger hamper shows you value that loyalty.

Gift or bonus?

Both, if you can. A cash bonus helps with travel and school fees in January, while a hamper makes sure the family enjoys Christmas. If your budget allows only one, ask yourself what will make the biggest difference to that person this December.

Timing

  • Give it before they travel. Many household staff go home to their families for Christmas, so aim for the second or third week of December.
  • Deliver it to their family if they live elsewhere in Lagos. It saves them carrying a heavy hamper on public transport.
  • Treat everyone fairly. Staff talk to each other, so keep gifts consistent by role and length of service.

Frequently asked questions

What should I give my driver for Christmas in Nigeria?

A foodstuff hamper for his family, ideally with a cash bonus. A staff pack or the Chicken and Jollof Box is practical and appreciated.

Should I give household staff a hamper or cash?

Both, if possible. Cash helps with January expenses, while a hamper takes care of the family's Christmas. If you choose one, think about what helps that person most.
