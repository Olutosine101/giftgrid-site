How Much Does a Christmas Hamper Cost in Nigeria? 2026 Price Guide

GiftGrid · November 17, 2026

Ask five vendors what a Christmas hamper costs and you will get five very different answers. That is because "hamper" can mean anything from a few biscuits in a basket to a 25kg bag of rice with everything a family needs for the season. This guide explains what you can expect at each budget in Lagos this year, what pushes the price up, and how to get the most from what you spend.

What actually drives the price

  • The size of the rice bag. Rice is usually the single most expensive item. Moving from 5kg to 10kg, or to a 25kg bag, changes the price more than anything else.
  • Oil in litres. Vegetable oil is the next biggest cost, so 1L, 2L, 3L and 5L hampers sit at very different prices.
  • Protein. A whole frozen chicken, stockfish or dried fish adds real value, and needs cold or careful packing.
  • Brands. Named brands cost more than unbranded alternatives, but the person receiving the hamper knows exactly what they are getting.
  • Packaging. Baskets, crates and wrapped boxes all cost money. A good hamper puts most of the budget into what is inside.
  • Delivery. In Lagos, delivery depends on distance. Ours starts from ₦2,000 and is shown at checkout before you pay.

How to get more for your money

  • Compare what is inside, not the basket. Ask for the exact list with sizes: "rice" could mean 1kg or 10kg.
  • Choose staples over filler. Seasoning, tomato paste and spices cost little but make a hamper usable on day one.
  • Order early. Prices for rice, oil and chicken often climb in December. Ordering in November locks in today's price and gives you the best delivery slots.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good budget for a Christmas hamper in Nigeria?

For family and friends, ₦50,000 to ₦100,000 buys a genuinely useful cooking hamper. Under ₦50,000 works for a smaller pantry pack, and ₦200,000 and above buys a full store-up for a household.

Why are some Christmas hampers so expensive?

Mainly the size of the rice bag and the litres of oil, then protein like whole chicken, the brands used and the packaging. Always ask for the item list with sizes so you can compare fairly.

When should I order a Christmas hamper in Lagos?

As early as you can. For delivery on 24 December, order by 12 noon on 23 December. Corporate and bulk orders should be confirmed by 11 December.
