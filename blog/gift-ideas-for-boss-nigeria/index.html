Gift Ideas for Your Boss in Nigeria (Without It Being Awkward)

GiftGrid · November 17, 2026

Giving a gift to your boss can feel like a minefield. Spend too much and it looks like you want something. Spend too little and it looks like an afterthought. The answer is a gift that is thoughtful, useful and clearly about appreciation. Here is how to get it right.

Give as a team when you can

A gift from the whole team is the least awkward option. Nobody stands out, everyone contributes what they can, and the combined budget buys something genuinely nice. Ask one person to collect contributions and place the order.

For a big occasion

For a milestone birthday, a promotion or a farewell, a premium hamper from the whole team makes the moment feel significant.

Executive Hamper · ₦158,500Ferrero Rocher, Nescafé Gold, cashews, honey and wine (18+).

Budget guide

  • From you personally: keep it modest, around ₦35,000 to ₦55,000. A fruit basket or a coffee box is plenty.
  • From a small team: ₦55,000 to ₦75,000 for a coffee box or fruit crate.
  • From a department: ₦100,000 and above for an executive hamper or a custom box.

The right occasions

  • Birthday: the most natural moment for a team gift. Have it delivered to the office in the morning.
  • Christmas and end of year: a good time to say thank you for the year.
  • Promotion or new role: congratulate them on the step up.
  • Farewell: a send-off gift from the team, with a card everyone signs.

What to avoid

  • Anything too personal, like clothes, perfume or jewellery.
  • Cash or gift cards, which can feel transactional.
  • Alcohol, unless you are certain they drink.
  • Expensive solo gifts that could make colleagues uncomfortable.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good gift for a boss in Nigeria?

A coffee and tea box, a fresh fruit crate or a treat box to share with the office. For bigger occasions, a premium hamper from the whole team.

Is it okay to give my boss a gift on my own?

Yes, as long as it is modest and professional. A team gift is usually less awkward and lets you give something bigger together.
