Housewarming Gift Ideas in Lagos: What to Bring to a New Home

GiftGrid · November 17, 2026

Moving house in Lagos is exhausting: agents, landlords, movers and traffic, then unpacking in a home with an empty kitchen. A good housewarming gift solves a problem for the new owner. Here are the gifts that people are actually glad to receive.

Fill the empty kitchen

The first week in a new home usually means takeaways because the kitchen is not stocked. A provisions hamper changes that immediately, and it is the most useful housewarming gift you can give.

Family Care & Support · ₦85,000Rice, oil, Semovita, eggs, fish, crayfish, tomato paste, seasoning and more.
Breakfast Provisions Box · ₦51,000Everything for breakfast: cereal, oats, milk, Milo, tea, sugar and spread.

Bring something for the party

If there is a housewarming gathering, a gift the host can share is perfect. A fruit basket looks beautiful on the table, and a snack crate keeps guests happy.

Harvest Abundance Basket · ₦35,000Pineapple, pawpaw, watermelon, grapes, oranges, bananas, apples, dates and juice.
Sweet Tooth & Craving · ₦40,000Chocolates, doughnuts, chin-chin and nuts for the guests.

For the coffee lover's new space

A home office or a new kitchen counter is the perfect place for a coffee and tea box.

Coffee & Tea Connoisseur · ₦55,000Nescafé Gold, cappuccino, Coffee-mate, Lipton tea and biscuits.

Budget guide

  • Under ₦50,000: a fruit basket or a sweet treat box.
  • ₦50,000 to ₦70,000: a coffee and tea box or breakfast provisions.
  • ₦85,000 and above: a full home hamper. See more ideas under gifts under ₦50,000 if you are on a tighter budget.

Tips for a housewarming gift

  • Skip decor unless you know their taste. Food and drink always fit.
  • Deliver after the move, not on moving day. A day or two after they have the keys is ideal.
  • Check the new address carefully. New estates and streets can be hard to find, so include a landmark and a phone number.
  • Add a note wishing them peace and good things in the new home.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good housewarming gift in Nigeria?

A provisions or foodstuff hamper is the most useful, because new homes rarely have a stocked kitchen. A fruit basket or snack box is great if there is a party.

When should I send a housewarming gift?

A day or two after the move, once they have settled in. Moving day itself is usually too hectic.

Can you deliver to a new estate in Lagos?

Yes. Add a clear landmark and the recipient's phone number at checkout so the rider can find the new address easily.
