What to Put in a Christmas Hamper in Nigeria: The Complete Checklist

GiftGrid · November 17, 2026

The best Christmas hampers in Nigeria are the ones that get used. Nobody remembers a basket of cellophane and filler, but everybody remembers the hamper that cooked their Christmas rice. Use this checklist whether you are building a hamper yourself or checking what a vendor is offering.

1. The staples

These carry most of the value and most of the cost. Pick the sizes to match your budget.

  • Rice: 5kg for a small hamper, 10kg for a family, 25kg for a store-up
  • Vegetable oil: 1L to 5L
  • Spaghetti or macaroni
  • Noodles: a few packs, or a full carton
  • Semovita, garri or another swallow
  • Beans, especially honey beans (oloyin)

2. The cooking basics people forget

This is the difference between a hamper that looks full and one that is ready to cook with. These items are small and affordable, but leaving them out means a trip to the market on Christmas Eve.

  • Tomato paste or a tomato mix
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Salt
  • Curry and thyme
  • Ground pepper
  • Party jollof seasoning, if you want to be specific about the menu

3. The breakfast shelf

  • Milk: tin or sachet
  • A chocolate drink like Milo
  • Tea bags
  • Sugar
  • Oats, cornflakes or Golden Morn

4. Drinks

  • A 1L carton of juice
  • Malt or soft drinks for the children and guests
  • Wine only if you know the person drinks; it must go to adults (18+)

5. Treats

  • Biscuits and cookies
  • Chocolates
  • Chin-chin or plantain chips
  • Cashew nuts or groundnuts

6. Protein (optional, but loved)

  • A whole frozen chicken, packed cold
  • Stockfish, dried catfish or crayfish
  • Eggs, carefully packed in a crate

What to leave out

  • Anything close to its expiry date. Check dates on every item, especially biscuits and drinks.
  • Fresh items without a cold chain. Chicken and fish need to stay frozen until delivery.
  • Random filler. Plastic decorations and tiny sample sizes make a hamper look bigger but add little.
  • Alcohol for someone who does not drink. When in doubt, choose juice.

Packing tips

  • Put heavy items like rice and oil at the bottom, and breakables like eggs at the top.
  • Use a sturdy box or bag; large hampers can weigh over 40kg.
  • Add a handwritten note. It is the part people keep.

Frequently asked questions

What is usually inside a Nigerian Christmas hamper?

Rice, vegetable oil, spaghetti, noodles and a swallow like Semovita, plus tomato paste, seasoning, salt and spices. Many hampers add milk, Milo, tea, sugar, juice, biscuits and sometimes a whole chicken.

Should I put wine in a Christmas hamper?

Only if you know the person drinks, and only for adults (18+). Juice or malt is the safer choice for families and for people you do not know well.
