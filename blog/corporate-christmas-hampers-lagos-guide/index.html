Corporate Christmas Hampers in Lagos: A 2026 Buyer's Guide

GiftGrid · October 22, 2026

Corporate Christmas hampers are the one gift your whole team remembers into the new year. Done well, they say thank you for the year's work. Done late, they arrive in January with half the items missing. This guide covers how to plan corporate Christmas hampers in Lagos: budget, contents, branding, delivery and timing.

1. Set a budget per person, not a total

Start with what each hamper should cost, then multiply. Most Lagos companies spend between ₦35,000 and ₦90,000 per staff hamper, with client hampers at the top of that range. Decide early whether everyone gets the same box or whether managers, top performers and key clients get a step up.

  • Around ₦38,000: a practical foodstuff hamper such as the Staff Appreciation Box.
  • ₦50,000 to ₦65,000: a coffee and treats box or an exotic fruit hamper for clients.
  • ₦85,000 and above: a full family foodstuff hamper for senior staff and long-standing partners.

2. Choose items people actually use

The best Christmas hampers in Nigeria are generous and practical. Rice, vegetable oil, seasoning and drinks get used over the holidays. Novelty items get forgotten. Mix staples with one or two treats so the hamper feels like a gift, not a ration.

Staff Appreciation Box · ₦38,000Rice, oil, Chivita, Pringles, Knorr, Gino and a mini yam. Our most-ordered staff hamper.

3. Brand it lightly

A note card with your logo and a short message from leadership does more than branded packaging. It is personal, costs little and does not end up in the bin. Write one message for staff and another for clients.

4. Plan delivery before you order

Decide whether hampers go to the office for a hand-out day or straight to each person's home. Home delivery across Lagos means a list of names, phone numbers and full addresses, so start collecting it now. Add a backup contact for each address, such as gate security or a family member.

5. Order early: the dates that matter

December traffic in Lagos is real, and the best items sell out. Confirm corporate orders by 11 December and plan deliveries for the second and third weeks of December, before people travel.

  1. Early November: agree the budget and the list of recipients.
  2. Mid November: choose boxes and get a quote.
  3. By 11 December: confirm the order and pay the invoice.
  4. Mid December: deliveries go out.
