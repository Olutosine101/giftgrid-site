Graduation Gift Ideas in Nigeria: Convocation, NYSC and New Jobs

GiftGrid · November 17, 2026

Graduation in Nigeria is often a series of milestones: final exams, convocation, NYSC and, finally, the first job. Each stage comes with its own needs, and the best gift for a graduate fits where they are right now. Here are ideas for every step.

For final exams and convocation

After years of hard work, a celebratory treat is perfect. Have it delivered on the morning of convocation, or the day the last exam ends.

Sweet Tooth & Craving · ₦40,000Ferrero Rocher, Kinder Bueno, doughnuts, chocolate strawberries and chin-chin.
The Continental Royalty · ₦48,000A celebration breakfast delivered on the big day.

For NYSC

A corps member starting their service year, often in a new city, needs to stock a new room on a small allowance. A pantry pack is exactly the right gift. If they are serving in Lagos, we can deliver straight to their lodge or new home.

Staff Starter Pack · ₦34,000Rice, oil, spaghetti, noodles, tomato mix, seasoning, salt, curry and pepper.
Breakfast Provisions Box · ₦51,000Cereal, oats, milk, Milo, tea and sugar for early CDS mornings.

For the first job

Long days and early starts call for coffee. A coffee and tea box for their desk or new apartment is useful from day one.

Coffee & Tea Connoisseur · ₦55,000Nescafé Gold, cappuccino, Coffee-mate, Lipton tea and biscuits.

From parents and family

For a son or daughter moving into their first place, a full provisions hamper is a practical way to say "we're proud of you".

Family Care & Support · ₦85,000Rice, oil, Semovita, eggs, fish, crayfish and seasoning.

Budget guide

  • From a friend: ₦34,000 to ₦40,000 for a pantry pack or treat box.
  • From family: ₦51,000 to ₦85,000 for breakfast provisions, a coffee box or a home hamper.
  • Group gift: friends can pool money for a bigger celebration.

Tips

  • Match the gift to the stage: celebration for convocation, practical for NYSC and a first job.
  • Add a proud note. Graduates keep those.
  • Check the address, especially for hostels, lodges and new apartments. Include a phone number.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good graduation gift in Nigeria?

A treat box or celebration breakfast for convocation, a pantry pack for NYSC, and a coffee box for a first job. Match the gift to the stage.

What should I give a corps member?

Something practical for their new room: a pantry pack with rice, oil, pasta, noodles and seasoning, or breakfast provisions.

Can you deliver to a hostel or NYSC lodge in Lagos?

Yes, within our Lagos delivery areas. Include a landmark and the graduate's phone number so the rider can reach them.
