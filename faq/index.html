Ordering and delivery

What is GiftGrid?

GiftGrid is a Lagos gifting company. We curate premium gift boxes and hampers with products Nigerians love, pack them by hand and deliver them on the date you choose.

Where do you deliver?

Across Lagos, to 51 areas. See delivery areas and fees.

How much is delivery?

It depends on the area and is shown at checkout before you pay, starting from ₦2,000.

How far ahead should I order?

Order by 9:00 a.m. the day before delivery. For corporate orders, give us at least a week.

How do I pay?

Online with card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack, or ask us on WhatsApp.

Can I add a message?

Yes. Add a gift card message at checkout and we write it on a card inside the box.

Can I build my own box?

Yes. Curate a custom box on WhatsApp: tell us the occasion and budget and we put it together.

Do you do corporate and bulk orders?

Yes. See corporate gifting for staff hampers, client gifts and volume pricing.

Can I send a gift from abroad?

Yes. See sending gifts to Lagos from the UK, US and Canada.

What if the recipient is not home?

Add a backup contact at checkout, such as gate security or a second number. The rider calls them if the recipient is unavailable.
